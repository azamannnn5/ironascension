Categories

The 7 categories are fixed slots. You can rename and describe each one, but not add or remove them. Renaming updates the name everywhere it is listed on the site.

Payment Methods

The options in the cart's payment dropdown, and what customers see once they pick one.

Methods and instructions

Use the arrows to change the order. The instructions (for example your Cashapp tag or Zelle email) appear under the dropdown when the customer picks that method, on the order confirmation, and in their confirmation email. A method named "Bitcoin" automatically gets the Bitcoin discount below.

Bitcoin discount

Applied automatically at checkout when the customer picks Bitcoin.

Discounts

Real checkout math, not just display text. Changes apply to the next order immediately.

Shipping & Gifts

Thresholds check the cart total after discounts, in whole dollars. Orders below the free shipping amount pay the flat shipping fee. Crossing the free cap amount adds a free cap at checkout.

Shipping note

Emails

The message customers see after checkout, and the optional emails you can send when you move an order to a new status. Emails need the RESEND_API_KEY set in Netlify.

Order confirmation message

Shown on the confirmation screen after checkout and at the top of the customer's confirmation email. You can use {name}, {order_number} and {total}.

Order status emails

When you change an order's status in Orders, the admin asks whether to email the customer. It only offers this for statuses you turn on here. Placeholders: {name}, {order_number}, {total}, {payment_method}.

Shipping & Returns Text

The body of the Shipping & Returns page, written as plain text. The contact box at the bottom stays automatic.

Formatting: start a line with ## for a section heading, ### for a sub heading, - for a bullet. Put **double stars** around words to bold them. A blank line starts a new paragraph. The first paragraph is the intro.
Saving with the box empty switches back to the built-in text.
