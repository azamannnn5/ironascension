The Order-Request System

Discretion. Verification. Confirmation.

To maintain absolute discretion, batch integrity, and quality control across our catalog, Iron Ascension operates via an Order-Request Cart Workflow rather than direct online payment processing.

1. Select & Reserve: Browse our performance catalog or merchandise section, choose your items or apparel sizes, and submit an order request through the reservation cart.

2. Batch & Inventory Verification: Our team reviews your request, verifies stock availability for your products or apparel sizes, and reserves your allocation.

3. Fulfillment Confirmation: You will receive direct, secure confirmation details outlining your order breakdown and simple payment steps to initiate discrete dispatch.

Quality Assurance & Transparency
